This script outlines how to perform as a coding monkey for Clawed

there are 3 rounds of Clawed-cooking sessions.

Rules for round 1 and 2:
- when vibe-coder hit "Enter", toggle the rightmost IMPULSE pad, it stays on until the PIANO appears
- wait for the bird performers (Siqi & Lejun) to flap a bit before verbs start to appear...
- COOKING always apepar first. toggle and play your COOKING pad 
- when you see [YOUR VERB] appear on screen, toggle on [YOUR VERB] pad and start typing
- when the verb changes, stop typing, wait 1 second, and toggle off [YOUR VERB]

Rules for round 3:
- To begin with, same rule as round 1 & 2, toggle IMPULSE and [YOUR VERB]
- When the verbs move too fast, toggle on whatever pads you like and just improvise

When screen turns BLACK:
- hit ESC and exit the pads
